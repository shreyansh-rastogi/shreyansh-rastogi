# Hi, I'm Shreyansh Rastogi 👋

3rd-year Electronics & Communication Engineering student at **BMS College of Engineering**, Bengaluru, focused on **VLSI Design and Verification**.

I like turning specifications into RTL, and then spending an unreasonable amount of time proving it actually works. Waveforms are my favourite kind of art. 📈

---

## 🔧 What I Do

- **RTL Design:** Writing and simulating digital hardware in Verilog HDL.
- **Design Verification:** Building testbenches and checking that designs behave the way the spec says (not just the way I hoped).
- **Serial Communication Protocols:** Working with interfaces like QSPI, where timing and signalling details matter.
- **Scripting & Programming:** Using Python and C++ to support engineering and simulation work.
